Pets

You take one pet into a dungeon. Its bag is the loot you can carry out. In a fight, tap an ability on your turn when it is ready. Abilities start on cooldown. The wait ticks down once each fight round. Pet hits use your Accuracy, not your Strength or Intelligence.
