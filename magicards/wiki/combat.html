Fight

Attacks, shields, buffs & debuffs

These are the combat effects you see on cards and on the fight bar. Amounts change by card and stack — this page explains what each one does.

Attacks Shields Buffs Debuffs

Attacks

Attack cards deal one of these damage types. Matching shields can absorb a hit before it reaches life. Piercing and damage-over-time ignore shields.

Attack

Physical

A physical hit. Physical shields absorb it first, then Physical and Magical shields. Magical-only shields do not stop it.

Attack

Physical Piercing

A physical hit that ignores every shield and goes straight to life.

Attack

Magical

A magic hit. Magical shields absorb it first, then Physical and Magical shields. Physical-only shields do not stop it.

Attack

Magical Piercing

A magic hit that ignores every shield and goes straight to life.

Attack

Poison

Damage over time that hits life. Shields do not absorb it.

Attack

Burn

Fire damage over time that hits life. Shields do not absorb it.

Attack

Bleed

Wound damage over time that hits life. Shields do not absorb it.

Shields

Defense cards and some relics or buffs give you a shield. It soaks matching hits, then leftover damage goes to life. Damage Immunity can still save your life after shields are hit.

Shield

Physical

Absorbs Physical attacks. Magical and piercing hits ignore it.

Shield

Magical

Absorbs Magical attacks. Physical and piercing hits ignore it.

Shield

Physical and Magical

Absorbs both Physical and Magical attacks after the matching single shield. Piercing still ignores it.

Buffs

Helpful statuses on you — extra damage, shields, heals, or cleanses. Many fade over turns or are used up when they trigger.

Buff

Double Next Damage

Doubles the damage of your next attack, then is used up. Does not stack.

Buff

Next Attack Piercing

Your next Physical or Magical attack ignores shields (it becomes piercing). Used up on that attack. Does not stack.

Buff

Keep Defense

Leftover shields stay into the next turn instead of being cleared. Each stack covers one of your turns.

Buff

Physical Defense Aura

At the end of the turn, grants Physical shield equal to its stacks, then loses a stack. A fight pact can keep granting this for the whole fight without losing stacks.

Buff

Magical Defense Aura

At the end of the turn, grants Magical shield equal to its stacks, then loses a stack.

Buff

Defense Conversion

While it lasts, every shield you gain becomes Physical and Magical. Loses a stack when you act. When it runs out on you, you get No Defense.

Buff

Physical Damage Reduction

Lowers every incoming Physical hit — including piercing — by the current stacks. Hits do not use it up. Fresh amounts add together. Loses a stack at the start of your turn. Does not help against poison, burn or bleed.

Buff

Magical Damage Reduction

Lowers every incoming Magical hit — including piercing — by the current stacks. Hits do not use it up. Fresh amounts add together. Loses a stack at the start of your turn. Does not help against poison, burn or bleed.

Buff

Damage Immunity

Incoming damage cannot take your life. Shields can still be hit. Loses a stack at the start of your turn.

Buff

Retaliate

When a Physical or Physical Piercing hit reaches your life, you deal half of that life damage back (at least 1). One stack is used per hit. Does not fade on its own.

Buff

Magic Reflector

When a Magical or Magical Piercing hit reaches your life, you deal half of that life damage back (at least 1). One stack is used per hit. Does not fade on its own.

Buff

Lifesteal

When you deal damage to life — not shields — you heal that much, up to your remaining stacks. Those stacks are used up.

Buff

Double Heal

Doubles the next heal you receive, then is used up. Does not stack. Sickness still blocks the heal.

Buff

Regeneration

At the start of your turn, heals 1 life per stack, then loses a stack. Sickness blocks the heal.

Buff

Battle Focus

Draws one extra card at the start of each of your actions, then loses a stack. Playing it again replaces the old one.

Buff

Card Copy

The next card you play that can be copied is cloned into your hand. Each stack is one clone. Skips Card Cloner itself and single-use cards. Used up on that play. Soul Anchor does not freeze it.

Buff

Soul Anchor

Freezes the fight buffs you already have. Frozen buffs still work, but they cannot fade or be stripped. Debuffs, Card Copy, Candle Life, and dungeon-long effects are left alone. When Soul Anchor ends, the frozen buffs are removed.

Buff

Candle Life

Your attack hits apply Burn to the enemies they hit. You also gain Burn at the start of your turn. Lasts the whole fight. Soul Anchor does not freeze it.

Buff

Poison Antidote

Removes poison stacks equal to the antidote amount. Cards that grant this usually clear all poison.

Buff

Paralysis Antidote

Removes paralysis.

Buff

Burn Antidote

Removes burn stacks equal to the antidote amount. Cards that grant this usually clear all burn.

Buff

Bleed Antidote

Removes bleed stacks equal to the antidote amount. A Bandage from the item drawer clears all bleed.

Buff

Remove Vulnerable

Removes Vulnerable.

Buff

Remove Weakness

Removes Weakness.

Buff

Remove Confusion

Removes Confusion.

Buff

Remove All Debuffs

Clears every debuff on the target.

Buff

Hatching

Countdown on a dragon egg. The egg’s own behaviour counts it down and hatches the dragon. It is not a normal fading buff, and Remove All Buffs leaves it alone.

Debuffs

Harmful statuses. Some damage you each turn, some stop cards or heals, some strip your defenses.

Debuff

Poison

At the end of the turn, deals damage to life, then loses a stack. Shields do not absorb it.

Debuff

Burn

At the end of the turn, deals its full stacks as damage to life, then is removed. Shields do not absorb it.

Debuff

Bleed

At the end of the turn, deals damage to life equal to its stacks. Does not fade on its own. Use a Bandage or Bleed Antidote to clear it. Shields do not absorb it.

Debuff

Paralysis

You cannot play Attack cards this turn. Enemies with it skip their attack. Does not stack. Fades at the end of the turn.

Debuff

Confusion

On you only. Each card you play has a 50% chance to fizzle: the card is spent and still costs mana, but does nothing. Fades at the end of your turn.

Debuff

Weakness

Your attacks deal less damage. Fades at the end of your turn. If the target is also Vulnerable, the two cancel on that hit.

Debuff

Vulnerable

You take more damage from Physical and Magical hits, including piercing. Fades at the end of your turn. Weakness on the attacker cancels this on that hit.

Debuff

Magic Ink

The next Magical or Magical Piercing hit against the marked target deals more damage, then this is used up. Does not stack. Also fades at the end of the turn if unused.

Debuff

Sickness

Blocks all healing — potions, cards, pets and buffs. Fades at the end of the turn.

Debuff

No Defense

Blocks every way of gaining shields. Fades at the end of the holder’s turn. Does not last the whole fight.

Debuff

Corrosion

Eats the next shields the target gains, starting with Physical, then Magical, then Physical and Magical. Used up by that amount. Does not fade on its own.

Debuff

Panic

Enemies may skip their action. Does not apply to you. Fades at the end of their turn.

Debuff

Fire Landmine

When you play an Attack card, you gain Burn. Fades at the end of your turn.

Debuff

Soul Burn

At the end of each turn, loses a portion of max life as burn damage. Lasts for the fight.

Debuff

Blood Tithe

When it starts, you lose that life once and your cards cost less mana. Then you lose a portion of max life at the start of each of your turns. Lasts the whole fight.

Debuff

Remove All Buffs

Strips fight buffs from the target. The hatch countdown on a dragon egg is left alone.

Debuff

Remove Lifesteal

Removes Lifesteal stacks equal to the amount.
